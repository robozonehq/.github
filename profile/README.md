RoboZone is a student-led nonprofit introducing students in **grades 4–8** to robotics, programming, and engineering. Through an **eight-week curriculum** and mentor-led workshops, students learn to build circuits, write code, and design parts.

We want students to leave with a project they understand, the confidence to troubleshoot it, and the curiosity to keep building.

## Our impact

| Students taught | Workshop hours | Curriculum |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **90+** | **160** | **8 weeks** |

## What we teach

| Area | What students practice |
| --- | --- |
| **Arduino & electronics** | Building circuits and connecting hardware with code. |
| **C++ programming** | Writing, testing, and debugging programs for physical devices. |
| **CAD & engineering design** | Turning ideas into models, testing designs, and improving them. |

Our workshops bring these skills together through hands-on robotics projects, with activities at different difficulty levels.

## On GitHub

This organization is the home for RoboZone's code and technical projects. Browse our [public repositories](https://github.com/orgs/robozonehq/repositories) and start with each project's README for its purpose, setup instructions, and contribution details.

## Get involved

- **Contribute:** Report a bug, improve documentation, or suggest a project idea in the relevant repository. For substantial changes, open an issue first so we can discuss the approach.
- **Mentor or partner:** Help students learn or collaborate on a workshop. Visit [our website](https://robozone.world) to connect with RoboZone.
- **Support our programs:** Give through our [HCB donation page](https://hcb.hackclub.com/donations/start/robozone).

Our educational programs have included a partnership with **Code Ninjas**.
